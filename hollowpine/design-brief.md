# Hollowpine — design brief

**Design read.** For someone browsing an unusual place to stay: a single
handbuilt treehouse. The register is calm, tactile, a little adventurous, and
quietly premium, like a good field guide.

**Concept spine.** The site IS the descent. You do not read about the treehouse,
you climb down through it: the visitor's scroll drops the camera from the sunlit
canopy deck to the mossy forest floor in one continuous move, and the chapters
are the floors you pass.

**Delivery tier.** cinema — carries the animated website (the scroll-scrub film
is the Tier-1 hero mechanic; surrounding chrome uses transform-only micro-motion).

**Animation mode: animated-website.**

**Journey shape:** single-shot — ONE continuous ~15s vertical crane descent,
scrubbed end to end, then sliced into five seam-locked legs cut from that single
take so every seam is two adjacent frames of the same film (no visible cut) while
each leg carries its own chapter.

**Journey (chapters):**
1. Canopy — the lookout deck high in the leaves (48 ft).
2. Bridge — the rope suspension crossing between two limbs.
3. Cabin — the warm lantern-lit room around the trunk (26 ft).
4. Understory — the ladder dropping through cool ferns.
5. Ground — boots on moss at the forest floor (0 ft).

**World grammar.** One enormous ancient tree, one handbuilt treehouse, one lens.
Deep shadowed forest interior, frame edges falling into darkness, a single strong
shaft of cool daylight from high above, damp weathered timber and moss. Locked
exposure and white balance, constant slow downward velocity, no cuts. Only the
depth changes, canopy to floor.

**Mobile framing.** Every focal point held in the center-safe band; lighter 720p
mobile encodes.

**Delivery budget.** Desktop clips <= 32 MiB (actual 27M), mobile <= 16 MiB (actual 12M).

**Locked palette.**
- Background `#0e1512` (near-black forest green)
- Panel `#131d18`
- Ink `#f3efe4` (bone)
- Muted `#aebaad` (lichen)
- Accent `#e8734d` (muted coral, the painted ladder rung)

**Locked type.** Bricolage Grotesque (display) + Inter Tight (body). No serif.

**Section plan.** Journey (scrub film) / Manifesto statement band / Levels
editorial divided list / Visit CTA panel / Footer. No consecutive layout repeats;
two eyebrows across five sections.

**CTA inventory.** One intent page-wide ("Plan a visit"), distinct interaction
identities: solid coral pill with lift, ghost outline pill, brand mark to-top.
