Current models

Six nameplates across three body styles, offered with petrol or fully electric power — and a John Cooper Works version of nearly all of them.

MINI Cooper 3-Door

The core car. Shortest wheelbase in the range, the most direct steering, and the only one offered as both a petrol Cooper S and a fully electric Cooper SE.
